Privacy Policy

Roman eBay Inventory Tracker

Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. Purpose

Roman eBay Inventory Tracker is designed to help the account holder organize, manage, and synchronize eBay inventory with Google Sheets.

2. Information Accessed

With the account holder's authorization, the application may access eBay listings, item details, SKUs, prices, inventory quantities, and relevant order or fulfillment information needed for inventory management.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to organize inventory, update spreadsheets, and support inventory synchronization.

4. Data Storage

Authorized inventory information may be stored in Google Sheets. Authorization tokens may be stored securely through Google Apps Script to maintain the connection. The application does not request or store the user's eBay password.

5. Information Sharing

The application does not sell personal information or use it for advertising. Data is processed through eBay and Google services as needed for the authorized inventory functions.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only as needed for the inventory management service. The account holder may revoke application access through eBay and request deletion of stored information.
